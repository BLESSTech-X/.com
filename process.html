Back to homeA CLEAR, COLLABORATIVE WORKFLOW

From first conversation to confident launch.

A transparent process helps you understand what happens next, what you need to provide and when decisions are required.

01

Discovery conversation

We learn about your business, audience, goals, existing tools, budget and must-have features. This helps establish what problem the project should solve.

Outcome: shared understanding of the goal.
02

Scope, proposal and plan

We agree on the deliverables, responsibilities, pricing, timeline and any dependencies before work starts. Anything outside the agreed scope is discussed first.

Outcome: a clear plan and agreed expectations.
03

Design and approval

We shape the structure and visual direction, then ask for feedback on the agreed review points before proceeding with the build.

Outcome: approved direction before full implementation.
04

Build and test

We implement the agreed features and check the experience on mobile and desktop. Where relevant, forms, checkout flows and payment steps are tested within the agreed environment.

Outcome: a reviewed solution ready for handover.
05

Launch, handover and support

We publish the project when launch requirements are met, explain how to use the relevant tools and confirm the support included in your package.

Outcome: a live solution and clear next steps.

Timeline depends on the agreed scope

Some projects can be completed in 7–14 days, while larger or more complex builds may take longer. The confirmed timeline is shared after requirements are reviewed.
